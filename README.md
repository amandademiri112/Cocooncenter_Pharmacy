## Key Insights
  
**Overall customer satisfaction**  
- The dataset contains 1,539 records, with customer ratings, prices and product codes available for all 1,539 records.
- The average customer rating is 4.78 out of 5, with a minimum rating of 1 and a maximum rating of 5.
- Overall, 96.0% of records have a positive rating of 4 or 5, while 2.3% have a rating of 1 or 2. 1.7% have a rating of 3.
- A total of 1,321 records have a five-star rating, representing 85.8% of all records.
- Missing demographic information is a significant limitation: 1,035 records (67.3%) have no available age group, while 1,119 records (72.7%) have no available recommendation value.
- There are no null customer ratings, so the overall rating baseline can be calculated using all 1,539 records.
    
**Brand performance**  
- Among brands with at least 20 reviews, Topicrem has the highest average customer rating, at 4.85 out of 5 across 295 records.
- Eucerin has the lowest average rating among brands with at least 20 reviews, at 4.61 out of 5 across 214 records.
- Bioderma has the largest representation in the dataset, with 602 records and an average customer rating of 4.85 out of 5.
- Several smaller brands achieve perfect average ratings of 5.00, but these results are based on very small samples.
   
**Product category performance**  
- Body Moisturisers is the dominant category, with 1,067 records and an average customer rating of 4.82 out of 5.
- Body Soothing Products achieves an average rating of 4.84 out of 5 across 68 records, making it a strong performer among categories with meaningful representation.
- Body Healing / Repairing Products averages 4.64 out of 5 across 258 records.
- Body Radiance / Well-Being has the lowest average rating across the listed categories, at 4.07 out of 5 across 15 records.
- Some smaller categories achieve averages of 5.00.
   
**Product popularity and customer feedback**  
- Bioderma Atoderm Ultra Nourishing Cream, 2×500 ml, is the most-reviewed product represented in the dataset, with 507 product reviews and an average customer rating of approximately 4.85 out of 5.
- Topicrem UH Body Milk, 2×500 ml, ranks second in product review volume, with 295 reviews and an average customer rating of approximately 4.85 out of 5.
- Eucerin products feature prominently among the highly reviewed products, but some have lower ratings than the leading Bioderma and Topicrem products.
- The negative-review extraction identifies 35 records rated 1 or 2. The complaint-keyword extraction identifies 17 records rated 1–3 that mention terms associated with damage, leakage, expiry, smell or delivery.
- Positive-review keyword searches identify examples of high-rated feedback mentioning quality, recommendations, moisturising and skin. These are useful for exploring customer sentiment, but keyword matches alone do not establish the overall reasons for satisfaction.
   
**Customer demographics**  
- Among reviewers with an available age group, the 45–54 age group has the highest average rating, at 4.88 out of 5 across 50 records.
- The 17-or-under group has the lowest average rating, at 4.33 out of 5, but contains only three records, so this result is not sufficiently robust for a strong conclusion.
- Male reviewers average 4.84 out of 5 across 57 records, compared with 4.76 out of 5 across 443 female-reviewer records.
- The available age-group recommendation data contains substantially more positive than negative recommendations. However, the missing recommendation information means the results cannot be treated as representative of all customers.
   
**Price and product quality**  
- The average listed product price is €16.99, the lowest price is €3.20, and the highest is €34.50.
- The price query can be used to identify expensive products and compare their average customer and overall product ratings. However, a higher price does not necessarily mean higher satisfaction or better value.
- The original product-quality query returns no rows because the quality scores are stored as decimal strings such as 4.9 and 5.0, rather than the exact strings 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5.
